# IoT Security Research

### Exploring IoT Security, Wireless Technologies & Hardware Hacking

Welcome to my **IoT Security Research** repository — a space dedicated to exploring the security of connected devices, wireless communication technologies, embedded systems, and the rapidly evolving Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem.

This repository documents my learning journey, technical research, practical experiments, and observations as I expand my knowledge beyond traditional cybersecurity into the world of hardware and IoT security.

My goal is to understand how connected technologies work, explore their potential security weaknesses, and develop the practical knowledge needed to build and secure more resilient systems.

---

## About My Research

The Internet of Things connects the physical and digital worlds through devices that collect, process, and exchange information. While this connectivity creates new opportunities, it also introduces security challenges involving communication, authentication, data protection, firmware, and device integrity.

My interest in this field comes from a desire to understand technology at a deeper level — not only how systems operate, but also how their underlying components interact and where security can be improved.

Through continuous learning and hands-on exploration, I aim to develop a stronger understanding of IoT security and the relationship between hardware, software, networks, and wireless communication.

## Research Areas

### Wireless Security
Exploring wireless communication technologies, their underlying principles, potential attack surfaces, and the security considerations involved in protecting connected systems.

### Embedded Systems Security
Learning how embedded systems operate, how their software and hardware interact, and how security can be incorporated throughout their design and implementation.

### Hardware Hacking
Developing practical knowledge of hardware architecture, electronic components, device interfaces, and hardware-related security concepts.

### IoT Vulnerability Research
Studying common security weaknesses in connected environments, understanding their potential impact, and exploring responsible methods of identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities.

### Security Analysis & Documentation
Recording technical observations, research findings, lessons learned, and security recommendations in a structured and transparent manner.

## My Approach

I believe meaningful security research begins with curiosity, experimentation, and a willingness to understand how things work beneath the surface.

My approach focuses on:

- **Continuous learning:** Building a strong foundation in IoT, wireless technologies, and embedded systems.
- **Hands-on exploration:** Applying theoretical knowledge through controlled and authorized experiments.
- **Technical analysis:** Understanding system behaviour, identifying potential weaknesses, and evaluating security implications.
- **Responsible research:** Respecting privacy, authorization, and the safety of other systems and users.
- **Knowledge sharing:** Documenting my learning and making technical concepts easier to understand.

I aim to distinguish verified findings from assumptions and to support technical conclusions with reliable evidence wherever possible.
