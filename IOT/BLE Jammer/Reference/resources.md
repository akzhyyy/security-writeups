# IoT, BLE, and Hardware Security Resources

This document collects technical references for my ongoing research into Bluetooth Low Energy, IoT security, embedded systems, and hardware hacking.

The resources are intended to support independent study and future security assessments.

---

## 1. Bluetooth Low Energy

### Bluetooth Core Specification

**Organization:** Bluetooth Special Interest Group (Bluetooth SIG)

**Resource:** https://www.bluetooth.com/specifications/specs/

**Purpose:**

The primary technical reference for Bluetooth architecture, protocol behavior, and security mechanisms.

**Research applications:**

- Understanding BLE architecture.
- Studying communication procedures.
- Reviewing security-related specifications.
- Establishing a technical basis for BLE assessments.

## 2. IoT Cybersecurity

### NISTIR 8228

**Title:** Considerations for Managing Internet of Things (IoT) Cybersecurity and Privacy Risks

**Organization:** National Institute of Standards and Technology

**Resource:** https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8228/final

**Purpose:**

Provides guidance on understanding and managing cybersecurity and privacy risks associated with IoT devices.

### NISTIR 8259

**Title:** Foundational Cybersecurity Activities for IoT Device Manufacturers

**Organization:** National Institute of Standards and Technology

**Resource:** https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8259/final

**Purpose:**

Describes foundational cybersecurity activities relevant to IoT device manufacturers.

**Research applications:**

- Understanding device security capabilities.
- Studying security requirements.
- Evaluating secure design principles.
- Identifying areas for defensive improvement.

## Research Principles

When using these resources:

1. Prefer primary specifications and authoritative documentation.
2. Record the exact document version when relevant.
3. Distinguish documented protocol behavior from implementation-specific behavior.
4. Verify security claims before including them in a report.
5. Keep experimental evidence separate from theoretical analysis.
6. Follow applicable legal and ethical requirements.
